subject: who i am (the real story) [ pinned post ]
mood: determined music: something only i can hear location: his bedroom window

Everyone at Shoalsburg High thinks they know the story. Poor Fatima, dropped her books one day, got crushed at the Dynacorp site two weeks later, parents suing, very sad. They light candles for like a month and then forget.

They don't know the real story. The real story is that Ashby and I were always going to find each other. The accident wasn't the end of anything. It was the part where the universe finally said okay, now you can stop pretending you have anywhere else to be.

I've been keeping this journal so when we're together for real, he'll be able to read it and understand. Everything I did. Why I did it. He'll see it was all for him.

(He helped me pick up my books once. He bent down and his hair fell into his eyes and he laughed and said here and gave me back my copy of Wuthering Heights. That was the day I knew.)

her moves

Boxed text is the move as printed on the sheet. Everything under it in italics is mine.

Unresolved Traumathe Ghost — automatic
Whenever something brings to mind your death, you choke up and gain the Condition traumatized if you don't have it already. Whenever someone helps you resolve this Condition, you both mark experience.

The one move she didn't choose — the skin hands it to every Ghost. Note the exit: someone else has to help her resolve it, and the rules pay them both for it. Nobody ever did.

Projected Blamethe Ghost
While you've got the Condition traumatized, you may act as though others had the Condition at fault for my death.

The whole character in one move: a standing licence to treat whoever is nearest as her killer, available precisely whenever she is hurting. And she gets traumatized for free, from the move above, every time anything reminds her of how she died.

Creepthe Ghost
When you silently witness someone in one of their most private moments, perhaps sleeping or putting on makeup, gain a String on them.

It pays her a String for the thing she was going to do anyway. She had been in Ashby's bedroom while he slept before the campaign even started — that is written into her backstory.

Limitlessthe Ghost
You can walk through walls and fly.

Four words, and no door in Shoalsburg is closed to her. Held together with Creep, there is nowhere in town she cannot be and no reason not to be there.

Lureanother Skin — bought with an advance
Whenever someone makes a promise to you, they mark XP. Whenever someone breaks a promise to you, you mark XP.

Her one borrowed move, and the one she chose most deliberately. It pays her for being let down. For a girl whose entire identity is being wronged, it converts the grievance into advancement — she gets better at being a ghost every time someone fails her.

Sex Movethe Ghost
When you have sex with someone, you both get to ask a question of one another. This can be asked in character or player-to-player. They must answer honestly and directly.

Used once in play: Nov 6, with Ashby — the morning he woke up to her already there.

Note the shape of the four Ghost moves she took. Creep and Limitless are the voyeur's pair — no boundary holds. Unresolved Trauma and Projected Blame are the mean-and-spiteful pair — the grievance becomes a weapon aimed at whoever's nearest. She took all four. She took none of the moves that point toward giving, or healing, or being helped.

basic moves — as printed

The sheet, verbatim. Italics under a box are mine.

Turn Someone On−1
When you turn someone on, roll with Hot. On a 10 up, gain a String on them and they choose a reaction from below. On a 7-9, they can either give you a String or choose one of the reactions.
  • I give myself to you,
  • I promise something I think you want, or
  • I get embarrassed and act awkward.
For rules on asexuality and non-attraction, turn to page 49.

The move she needs most and rolls worst. At −1 she fails it more often than not — and every failure marks experience, which is the cruellest joke on the sheet: she advances by being rejected.

Shut Someone Down−1
When you shut someone down, roll with Cold. On a 10 up, choose one from below. On a 7-9, choose one from below, but you come across poorly, and they give you a Condition in return.
  • They lose a String on you,
  • If they have no Strings on you, gain one on them,
  • They gain a Condition, or
  • You take 1 Forward.
Keep Your Cool−1
When you keep your cool and act despite fear, name what you're afraid of and roll with Cold. On a 10 up, you keep your cool and gain insight: ask the MC a question about the situation and take 1 Forward to acting on that information. On a 7-9, the MC will tell you how your actions would leave you vulnerable, and you can choose to back down or go through with it.
Lash Out Physically+1
When you lash out physically, roll with Volatile. On a 10 up, you deal them harm, and they choke up momentarily before they can react. On a 7-9, you harm them but choose one:
  • They learn something about your true nature and gain a String on you,
  • The MC decides how bad the harm turns out,
  • You become your Darkest Self.

Oct 9, at the party: she sucked the breath out of Joey's lungs with this. Ashby lashed out at her in return and his claws went straight through — the one direction the move doesn't run.

Run Away+1
When you run away, roll with Volatile. On a 10 up, you get away to a safe place. On a 7-9, you get away but choose one:
  • You run into something worse,
  • You cause a big scene, or
  • You leave something behind.
Gaze Into the Abyss+2
When you gaze into the abyss, name what you're looking for and roll with Dark. On a 10 up, the abyss shows you lucid visions, and you take 1 Forward to addressing them. On a 7-9, the abyss shows you confusing and alarming visions, but you get your answer nonetheless.

Her best roll on the sheet by a full point. Note the 7-9: you get your answer nonetheless. The abyss never refuses her. It is the only thing in Shoalsburg that doesn't.

Experience
Whenever you fail a roll, mark experience.

When you spend a String on someone, choose one:

To learn about tempting someone, turn to page 26.

The rule Projected Blame runs on. It also names her exact problem: a Condition only lifts when the sufferer takes appropriate action. She never took any.

backstory, as chosen

  • Gina knows that Fatima is dead, and how she died. That is where Gina's two Strings come from — the only person in town holding the actual story.
  • Fatima has been inside Ashby's bedroom while he was sleeping. That is where one of her Strings on him comes from. It is also, per Creep, a move she is mechanically rewarded for repeating.

♱ darkest self

She becomes invisible, unnoticeable — no one can see her, feel her, or hear her voice. She can still affect inanimate objects, but that is her only avenue of communication. She escapes when someone acknowledges her presence and demonstrates how much they want her around.

The sheet names Ashby beside it. The exit condition for her worst state is the one thing she has no way to earn, from the one person who was never going to give it — so the Darkest Self is less a punishment than a description of her ordinary Tuesday.

  • cannot accept the fact that it was her failure to act

subject: the town (in case you don't know it) [ setting ]
mood: chilly music: wind across an empty quarry location: the rim, looking down

Shoalsburg is the kind of town where the most important thing about it is gone. The quarry used to be everything — paychecks, gossip, the way the dust got into your hair on a windy day. People stopped using it and the town started dying politely.

Then Dynacorp showed up to industrialize what was left. New site, new fencing, new humming under your feet at night. They built the place I died at.

The Harvest Ball got cancelled this year because Dynacorp pulled the sponsorship. (My parents are suing them. They won't drop it, no matter how much I ask. They think they're doing it for me. They're doing it for themselves.)

( I overheard Ms. Rosewater talking about Dynacorp once when I was where I shouldn't have been. Some of what she said matters. Some of it I'm still working on. )

subject: at the party (i finally got him alone) posted: oct 9 · 03:47 a.m.
mood: ELECTRIC music: the music of the spheres (he could hear it too) location: a dark corner, his lap

I want to remember every second of tonight forever, so I'm writing it all down.

before the party

  • spied on Ms. Rosewater (Dynacorp things — saving for later)
  • Kyle was being a teacher's-pet about the quarry. Gina was making a map from his notes. cute. useful.

the drive over

Ashby and Joey had just had sex. I could tell. They came out of Joey's house with that look. They offered me a ride. I convinced Ashby to spend the first part of the party with just me. Joey didn't like it. Joey doesn't get to have an opinion.

the corner

  • I hinted that Joey was cheating on him with Gina. He flinched. I felt it.
  • I told him I knew his secret. He got flustered. He started to change.
  • I led him to a dark corner. I rubbed his shoulders. I stroked his hair. I helped him come down.
  • It weirded him out that I don't have a scent. And it also calmed him down. Both at once. That's what we are.
  • He turned anyway. I sat on his lap.
and his ears were pointed and his teeth were long and i wasn't afraid because the only thing in the whole world that could hurt me wasn't his teeth, it was him looking away, and he wasn't looking away, he was looking RIGHT at me —

then joey came at us

Ashby and Joey were both under the influence of the music of the spheres (don't ask, I don't fully understand it either, but it was everywhere tonight). Joey came at us pissed. I sucked away his breath. Ashby attacked me — and his claws went right through me. Of course they did. He can't hurt me. Joey ran away.

Vesk was somewhere being a problem with drugs. Manuel was doing something with the slide of his trombone. The "Amazement Zone" was apparently underwhelming. None of it mattered.

He woke up to me. Naked. There. I had been there for hours.

I told him it was in his interest for us to be together, in some sense. He didn't disagree. He's a smart boy. He understands incentives.

and he didn't scream and he didn't run and that means he wanted it, that's what it means, that's what people not screaming means —

subject: a deal posted: ??:?? · ??
mood: finally music: none. perfect quiet. location: somewhere with no clocks

Bill took me to see the Celestials. He took me to see the dolls.

He offered me an arrangement. I gave him Manuel. He gave me a doll of Ashby. Animate. Not independent. Mine.

the player had said, before the campaign even started, that fatima's redemption arc would be her realizing that her actions — or more honestly her inactions — were her own responsibility. that the accident was an accident, that the spurning was a story she'd written, that she could let him go.

that is not the arc that happened.

the arc that happened is the one where every time the door of self-knowledge cracked open she pulled the doorknob off and put it in her pocket and walked further into the house.

I have what I wanted. I always knew I would. People said I was delusional. Look at me now.

( The campaign continued in Shoalsburg without Fatima and without Manuel. Two empty desks. The Harvest Ball still cancelled. Dynacorp still humming under the ground. A boy who can't quite remember the girl he kept seeing out of the corner of his eye. )
